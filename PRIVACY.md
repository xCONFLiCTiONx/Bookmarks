# Bookmarks Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** August 5, 2026

Bookmarks provides a high-performance navigation interface.

## 1. Access and Permissions
- **Bookmarks:** The extension requires permission to read your browser bookmarks to display them in the custom interface.
- **History:** The extension accesses your browser history to display "Recently Viewed" sites. 
- **Storage:** Used to save your pinned bookmarks and custom titles locally.

All data listed above is processed locally on your device and is never transmitted to our servers.

## 2. Third-Party Services
- **Favicons:** To display icons for your bookmarks, the extension requests favicons from Google's service (`www.google.com/s2/favicons`). During this request, only the domain of the bookmarked site is shared with Google.

## 3. Privacy
No analytics, user tracking, or third-party advertisements are included in this extension.
